Frequently asked questions

What does Stripe charge per transaction in the US?

+

2.9% + 30¢ per successful domestic card charge, plus 1.5% for international cards and 1% when currency conversion is needed. Stripe Billing adds 0.7% of billing volume on pay-as-you-go, and Stripe Tax Basic is 0.5% per transaction where you are registered.

What are Lemon Squeezy's fees?

+

5% + 50¢ per transaction, plus 1.5% for buyers outside the US, 1.5% for PayPal payments and 0.5% on subscription payments. It is a merchant of record, so sales tax and VAT handling are included.

Is Lemon Squeezy shutting down?

+

Lemon Squeezy closed new store sign-ups on November 16, 2026 and says it will stop processing payments, including renewals, on March 31, 2027. It points existing sellers to Stripe Managed Payments.

What is Stripe Managed Payments?

+

Stripe's merchant-of-record offering. It adds 3.5% per successful transaction on top of standard Stripe processing fees and handles indirect tax for you; Stripe Billing is billed separately for subscriptions.

Should a small SaaS use a merchant of record?

+

A merchant of record costs more per sale but takes over global sales tax and VAT registration, filing and much of the chargeback burden. Below a few thousand dollars a month, the time saved often outweighs the extra fees.
