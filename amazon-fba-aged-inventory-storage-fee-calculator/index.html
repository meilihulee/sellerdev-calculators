Frequently asked questions

What changes to FBA storage fees on February 1, 2027?

+

Amazon is eliminating the storage utilization surcharge and the aged inventory surcharge, and combining aged-inventory charges with the monthly storage fee into one age-based rate. Amazon says this means flat or lower fees for inventory that sells within six months and higher fees for aged inventory.

What are the 2027 unified storage rates for standard-size items?

+

For standard-size, non-dangerous goods from January to September: $0.78 per cubic foot for 0–180 days, $2.78 for 181–270 days, $6.68 for 271–365 days, $7.68 (or $0.78 + $0.30 per unit, whichever is greater) for 366–455 days, and $8.68 (or $0.78 + $0.35 per unit) for 456+ days. October to December adds $1.62 per cubic foot.

How was the aged inventory surcharge calculated in 2026?

+

Through January 31, 2027 Amazon charges the base monthly storage fee ($0.78/cu ft standard-size, $2.40 in Oct–Dec) plus a surcharge on units aged 181+ days: $0.50, $1.00 and $1.50 per cu ft for 181–270 days (apparel, shoes, bags, jewelry and watches exempt), $5.45–$5.90 for 271–365 days, and the greater of $6.90/cu ft or $0.30/unit (366–455 days) or $7.90/cu ft or $0.35/unit (456+ days).

How is inventory age measured?

+

Amazon takes an inventory snapshot on the 15th of each month and counts age first-in, first-out across its network. A removal or disposal order submitted by 11:59 p.m. PT on the 14th keeps those units out of that month's aged charge.

Which inventory is hit hardest by the 2027 change?

+

Units aged 181–210 days see the largest per-cubic-foot jump for standard-size items ($1.28 combined today vs $2.78 from February 2027). Apparel and other previously exempt categories also start paying an aged rate ($1.78/cu ft) from 181 days.

Does this calculator include dangerous goods or the low-inventory-level fee?

+

No. Dangerous goods have their own storage rates and the low-inventory-level fee depends on days of supply. Use Seller Central's Fee Change Comparison dashboard for SKU-level figures; this tool is an estimate.
