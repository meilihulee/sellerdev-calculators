Frequently asked questions

How much does eBay take from a sale in 2026?

+

In most categories eBay charges a 13.6% final value fee on the total amount of the sale up to $7,500 (2.35% on the portion above), plus $0.40 per order ($0.30 for orders of $10 or less). Some categories use different rates.

Does eBay charge its fee on shipping and sales tax?

+

Yes. eBay calculates the final value fee on the total amount of the sale, which includes the item price, shipping and handling charged to the buyer, and sales tax.

What fees does Amazon FBA charge per unit?

+

A referral fee (15% of the sale price in most categories, with a $0.30 minimum), the FBA fulfillment fee based on size and weight, the FBA fuel and logistics surcharge, monthly storage per cubic foot, and any inbound placement or shipping costs.

Is eBay or Amazon FBA more profitable?

+

It depends on the item. FBA usually wins on small, fast-moving products where Prime visibility lifts conversion; eBay often wins on heavy, slow-moving or used items where fulfillment and storage fees eat the margin. Run both scenarios here with your own costs.

Where do I find my exact FBA fulfillment fee?

+

Use Amazon's Revenue Calculator in Seller Central or your product's fee preview. Paste that number into the FBA fulfillment fee field for an exact comparison.
